# Functional Singularity

[Русская версия](../ru/07-functional-singularity.md) · [Project home](../../README.md)

**Functional singularity** is a project-specific, reversible operating state: the organism consistently satisfies the current environment's functional requirements and continues to improve internally without structural reproduction. This is **not** technological singularity or proof of general intelligence.

### Entry hypothesis
Requirements coverage and reliability remain above predeclared bounds on consecutive evaluation windows; no unresolved structural capability gap; learning or operational optimization remains possible.

### Exit
A new external requirement creates a reproducible capability gap that cannot be handled by policy adaptation. Genesis evaluates but reproduction is not automatic.

### Important
A stable score is not sufficient. Record task classes, environmental coverage, observation horizon and statistical uncertainty. Knowledge maturity M and generation G are independent dimensions.
