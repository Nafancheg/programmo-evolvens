# Genesis Co-evolution

[Русская версия](../ru/06-coevolution.md) · [Project home](../../README.md)

Genesis must be capable of assessing increasingly complex organisms. Its own evolution is **competence-triggered**, not tied to organism generation numbers.

## Competence check
Test the current Genesis against calibrated cases of known outcomes, adversarial cases, previously unseen workloads and historical decisions. A competence deficit triggers a candidate Genesis update.

## Meta-evolution without infinite regress
- Genesis candidate: changes its methods, models or assessment repertoire.
- External verification protocol: independent test harness, fixed acceptance rules and human approval, versioned separately.
- Candidate cannot rewrite its own acceptance rules during evaluation.
- Compare old and new Genesis on the same blinded assessment set; reject regressions in critical safety gates.
- A validated Genesis may still refuse organism reproduction.

The verification protocol itself can be improved through an explicit external change procedure, not through candidate self-certification.
